About Forkly

I built Forkly to practise shipping a complete mobile product, from the first onboarding screen to placing an order. It runs on Android, iOS and the web from one codebase.

React NativeExpo RouterZustandAsyncStoragereact-native-svg
  • Find somewhere to eatBrowse by category, search by name, then open a restaurant to see its popular dishes and full menu.
  • Make each dish yoursPick a size, add extras and set the quantity. The price updates as you choose.
  • Cart and checkoutEdit items right from the cart, choose cash or card, and follow your orders in one place.
  • Reserve without the hassleTell a restaurant when you’re coming. No specific table is held, the restaurant just knows to expect you.
  • Picks up where you left offYour cart, orders and account are saved on the device, so nothing is lost when the app closes.
  • Welcome flowA three-slide intro, then sign up or log in. It only shows the first time.
